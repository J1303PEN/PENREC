# PENREC17.2 — Product Publishing Workflow

- Links commerce products to releases managed in PENREC Studio.
- Replaces free-text format entry with a controlled format selector.
- Adds optional SKU and barcode fields.
- Adds optional stock quantity and shipping weight.
- Adds optional digital-file reference.
- Adds pre-order and availability dates.
- Shows stock, pre-order, or made-on-demand status in the public store.
- Keeps track-level ISRC optional.
- Includes an idempotent Supabase migration for the new product fields.
